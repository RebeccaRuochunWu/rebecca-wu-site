About me

Hi, I'm Rebecca.

I build AI products by day and chase light with a camera on weekends. It turns out they're the same job.

I graduated magna cum laude from the University of Chicago, where I studied computer science, data science and economics. My passion is to build products that make people's lives easier.

Today I'm building the AI video editor at OpusClip, after taking Agent Opus, our first video agent, from a blank PRD to ~1,000 beta users. Before that, as an AI product manager intern at Microsoft, I built a Chief of Staff Agent that turns Teams, Outlook and SharePoint into a digest of priorities and blockers.

How I work

From conversation to shipped product

The same three steps on every product I build.

Listen first

I start with the people. For Agent Opus, I recruited 50+ seed users and ran interviews on video quality, agent UX and editing workflows before writing the roadmap.

01

Prototype fast

I turn what users tell me into clickable prototypes, so we react to real screens instead of specs. The Agent Opus and Mapier Reply prototypes on this site started that way.

02

Ship it

Then I take it to production with engineering. Agent Opus reached ~1,000 beta users, and the auto sound-effects revamp shipped in about a month.
